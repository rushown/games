One in a

Krillion

Fifteen questions. Three lifelines. One shot at a krillion points. Climb alone or race a friend on one phone.

How to play

  • Answer 15 multiple-choice questions. Each one is worth more points, and they get harder as you climb.
  • Tap an answer, then Lock it in. A wrong answer, or running out of time, ends your climb.
  • Safe havens at question 5 (1,000) and question 10 (32,000): once you pass one, you can't fall below it.
  • Not sure? Walk away and keep everything you've won so far.
  • Lifelines, one use each: 50:50 removes two wrong answers, Crowd polls a studio audience (they're not always right), Swap trades the question for a new one.
  • Duel: two players take turns on one device, each climbing their own ladder with their own lifelines. Most points banked wins. Rematches keep a running total.
  • Questions you've seen are remembered, so you won't get repeats until you've been through the whole bank. Add your own in the Question bank.
