How to play

  1. Choose generations (and whether legendaries are in), then pick a mode. Images load from PokéAPI first so the reels run smoothly.
  2. Spin: each of the six stat slots rolls and lands on its own random Pokémon. Stats stay hidden.
  3. Lock one slot: tap the slot whose Pokémon fits that stat best (Blissey landing in HP, Regieleki in Speed…). You score that Pokémon's base stat for that slot.
  4. The unlocked slots roll again. Lock one per spin until all six are locked; the last slot locks itself.
  5. Reroll spins the open slots again without locking (solo modes, limited).
  6. The reveal shows your total, every spin with its stats, and the best possible locks on the same spins.
  7. Duel: both players get the same spins in the same order. P1 locks, passes the phone, then P2.
