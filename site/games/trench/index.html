How deep does your answer go?

Each dive is 7 prompts. Type one answer to each. Correct answers are ranked by how rare they are, and every point takes you 10 m deeper. A perfect dive reaches 7,000 m.

Spelling doesn't need to be perfect, and common alternative names count. An answer that isn't on the chart scores nothing. In a duel, if you both give the same answer, each of you gets half points.
