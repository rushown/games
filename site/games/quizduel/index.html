Quiz Duel

Name your groups, tick the subjects, and take turns against the clock. Questions keep coming group by group until you end the quiz. Pass one you don't know and the next group gets a shot.

How groups answer
Maths: 20 seconds, then 10 seconds for the group it's passed to. Every other subject: 18 seconds, then 10 seconds when passed. Turns: questions go to each group in order (A, B, C, A…) and mix the ticked subjects. Play goes on until you press End quiz. Points: 10 for a direct answer, 5 for a passed one. A wrong answer or running out of time passes it on.
